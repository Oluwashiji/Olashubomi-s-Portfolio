About

Some designers hand you a logo and call it done. I hand you a brand that works.

I'm Micheal, a Brand Identity & Experience Designer specialising in building complete visual systems from the ground up. Every colour, typeface, and layout decision I make is tied to one goal: making your brand impossible to ignore and impossible to forget.

I've built brand systems for wellness companies, hospitality platforms, and faith-based organisations, translating strategy into visuals that perform across every touchpoint: app screens, ad creatives, merchandise, packaging, and pitch decks.
